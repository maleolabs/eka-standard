# Knowledge Dimensions

Classification is a retrieval property; reclassification does not change
Identity (P15). One Artifact has one primary dimension plus optional secondary
dimension(s). The twelve dimensions:

1. **Product Intent** — vision, strategy, principles, non-negotiables. Very stable.
2. **Requirements** — requirement documents ("what must be built and why") and amendments. Stable.
3. **Architecture** — system descriptions, domain models, component boundaries. Stable.
4. **Decisions** — irreversible decisions (ADR) and reversible ones (decision log). Stable, cumulative.
5. **Specifications** — functional and non-functional (NFR) specifications, API, data. Stable when Approved.
6. **Standards & Guidelines** — engineering standards, conventions, definition of done. Stable, high governance.
7. **Operational Knowledge** — runbooks, deployment, migration, checklists. Medium (per-environment).
8. **Governance & Quality** — review findings, audits, quality gates. Cumulative.
9. **Planning Knowledge** — plan content (roadmap, milestone definition) and artifact relationships. Medium (commitment).
10. **Records** — release record, change log, historical snapshots. Immutable.
11. **Research** — investigation findings, technical research results. Cumulative.
12. **Vocabulary** — glossary, canonical terms, lifecycle model. Very stable.
