# Rule R1: Identity Uniqueness

## Definition

No duplicate (Namespace, Type, ID, InstanceVersion) may exist in the
repository. Two Artifacts are the same if and only if their Identity is the
same.

## Requirements

- No duplicate (Namespace, Type, ID, InstanceVersion) may exist in the
  repository.
- Two Artifacts are the same if and only if their Identity is the same.
