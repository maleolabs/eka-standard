# Planning (Stratum 3)

- **Stratum:** 3
- **Token families:** scp-, epc-, plan-, trc-
- **Knowledge Dimensions:** planning

## Purpose

Home of commitment knowledge — scope, epics, and plans that convert
requirements and decisions into an executable commitment.

## Produces

Scope Definition (scp-), Epic (epc-), Plan (plan-), Traceability Artifact
(trc-).

## Consumes

Discovery (requirements, intent); Architecture (decisions, specifications,
standards).

## Authority

Derives from Discovery and Architecture; constrains Execution, Operations.
