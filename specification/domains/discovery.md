# Discovery (Stratum 1)

- **Stratum:** 1 (highest authority)
- **Token families:** vis-, str-, req-, fnd-
- **Knowledge Dimensions:** intent, requirements, research

## Purpose

Home of intent, requirements, and research knowledge.

## Produces

Vision/Manifesto (vis-), Strategy (str-), Requirement/PRD (req-), research
findings (fnd-).

## Consumes

Nothing from other strata (nothing ranks above stratum 1); consumption is
within-domain.

## Authority

Root of the authority chain; constrains Architecture, Planning, Execution,
Operations.
