# Operations (Stratum 5)

- **Stratum:** 5 (lowest authority)
- **Token families:** run-, rel-, shr-
- **Knowledge Dimensions:** operations, records

## Purpose

Home of operational knowledge and records — runbooks and release records that
preserve what ran and how it runs.

## Produces

Runbook/Operational Guide (run-), Release Record (rel-), Sharing Object
(shr-; snapshot copy + pin-hash, see tokens/shr.md).

## Consumes

Execution (execution aggregate, review/session findings); Architecture
(standards).

## Authority

Derives from Execution (transitively all higher strata); constrains nothing.
